# Fluorescent AGEs detected by spectro-photofluorimetry

-> Advanced glycation end-products (AGEs) are of the mechanisms related to diabetic vascular complications.
-> AGEs are multiple and heterogeneous moieties, there is no universally accepted method to measure them for for clinical purposes.
-> The aim of this work was to study the utility of a simple fluorimetric assay as predictor of complications.
