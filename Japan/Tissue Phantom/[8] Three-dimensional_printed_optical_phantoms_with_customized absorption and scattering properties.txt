-> commercially avaialble thermoplastics provide only a small range of physiologically relevant absorption (ua) and reduced scattering (us') values.

-> customizable acrylonitrile butadiene styrene (ABS) filaments for dual extrusion 3D printing of tissue mimicking optical phantoms. 

-> ua and us' values were adjustable by incorporating nigrosin and titanium dioxide in the filament extrusion process.

-> a wide range of physiologically relevant optical properties was demonstrated with a average repeatability with 11.5% for ua and 7.71% for us'.

Filament fabrication
-> custom filament suitable for 3D printing was fabricated by co-extruding a mixture of commercially available natural color ABS pellets, (Filabot, Montpelier, VT) with custom absorption and scattering ABS "chips"

-> These chips are made by combining ABS with either absorbing dye nigrosin or the scattering agent titanium dioxide (TiO2), both of which have been used in other optical phantom recipes. 


## Method ##
-> Fabrication of TiO2 chips consisted of the following steps:
-> 20 g of ABS pellets were dissolved in 50 ml of acetone for at least 12 hours. 
-> in a separate continer, 0.2 g of TiO2 was mixed with 10 ul of tap water and sonicated for 10 minutes, with up to 5 additional minutes if heterogeneties were persistent.

-> 1000 ul of acetone was then added in 200 ul increments to prevent precipitation of TiO2. 
-> The TiO2 solution was gradually added to the ABS mixture and was stirred vigorously to create a homogeneous solution. 
-> The mixture was then poured into a 21x21 cm pan to a layer thickness of 1.5 mm and vacummed to remove air bubbles. 
-> Vacuuming consisted of an 80 kPa vacuum for 1 min followed by a reduced 40 kPa vacuum for 1-2 seconds.

-> This was repeated until the visible bubbles were eliminated.
-> The pan and ABS/TiO2 solution were left in the 40 kPa vacuum overnight to dry and solidify.
-> once completely solidifed, the thin sheet was cut with scissors into small chips approximately 2x2 mm in dimension.
-> Nigrosion chips were created using the same protocol described above but replacing TiO2 solution with a solution of 0.02g of nigrosin and 800 ul tap water. 

-> The ratio of nigrosin/TiO2 chips to ABS pellet in a specific filament was specified by mass ratio.



-> A commercially available pastic extruder (Wee Extruder, Filabot, Montpelier, VT) was used to extrude custom filaments. 

-> Each extrusion required a total mass of 30 g and the die temperature was set to 181.5 C. 

-> The Nigrosin and TiO2 chips were mixed together prior to extrusion in the desired mass ratio. 
-> Each was apportioned into smaller samples and then added to the hopper regularly throughout the extrusion process to provide a more homogeneous filament product.

-> Repeatability was demonstrated by making several cubes with the same recipe.
