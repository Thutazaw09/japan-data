## Scattering Property
-> Scattering of photons is a physical procedure greatly decided by wave characteristics of photons and structures of materials that they travel through, including particle size and shape and material's density.

# inelastic scattering
-> after interplaying with atoms or molecules in the medium, those photons lose or gain energy and subsequently change the frequency.

### Importanace notice
-> the calculation results based on the AD method are influenced by light loss owing to scattering through glass slides on sample holder and boundary of a sample.
-> To reduce the calculation error of IAD methods caused by light losss, the sample should be a flat slab and completely cover the port of the integrating sphere.
-> Moreover, the diameter of the incident beam should be not only small but also much smaller than the diameters of the entranc and exit ports of the integrating sphere.

# Monte Carlo 
-> MC simulation is an extensive class of computational algorithms that depends on repeated random sampling to obtain numerical results.
-> it mainly use numerical integration, optimization, and generating draws from probability distribution.
-> MC is valid for a wide range of absorption and scattering cofficients and photon paths.
-> It is usually used to simulate light propagation in turbid materials under assumption of uniform macroscopic optical properties over the ranges of ultraviolot, Vis/NIR light.
-> MC has high accuracy and slow compuataional time.
